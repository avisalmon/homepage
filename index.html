Evolution of Consciousness: From Selfish Genes to Selfish Minds

An intellectual journey from evolutionary theory to the shaping of human consciousness. Examines the speculative extension of Dawkins' "Selfish Gene" theory – whether consciousness configurations themselves can act as replicators. Explores how ideologies, worldviews, and cognitive styles compete like quasi-species in an ecosystem of minds, why societies polarize into two opposing camps, how toxic ideas spread like viruses, and introduces concepts like "cognitive immunity" against propaganda and de-radicalization methods in an era of misinformation and political extremism.

Bio-Convergence in Israel: Silicon Technologies Meet Biology

Executive summary on bio-convergence – the fusion of biology with engineering and electronics – as a powerful engine of medical innovation in Israel. Explores how semiconductors, sensors, and micro-mechanics merge with life sciences to create bio-electronic solutions for monitoring, diagnosis, and treatment, with focus on CMOS biosensors, lab-on-chip systems, implantable bioelectronics, and Israel's 80+ startups in this strategic growth area.
